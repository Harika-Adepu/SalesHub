# SalesHub

Link of Detailed View of the Project: [https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/149L0GlNxpn6HVuohWU0O3G3DWCtbfslj](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Pp-3mcoXPdtcHpID5pMhAS1HBWpvdVsM)

Sales Intelligence Hub is an interactive Power BI analytics solution designed to transform complex retail data into actionable business insights. By analyzing revenue across multiple cities and customer segments, this project provides a comprehensive view of operational performance and consumer behavior.  

🚀 What it Does

Revenue Analysis: Processes over 659K retail records to track total revenue and gross margins across various product lines.  
Trend Identification: Pinpoints peak sales hours and high-performing geographical regions to optimize staffing and inventory.  
Dynamic Exploration: Enables users to perform deep-dive analysis through interactive slicers and drill-down features for city-level or category-level specifics. 
Performance Tracking: Monitors hourly sales volatility and payment pattern preferences (e.g., E-wallet vs. Cash).  

🛠️ Technical Highlights

Data Modeling: Implemented a robust Star Schema to ensure efficient querying and data integrity across 659K+ records.  
Advanced Analytics: Developed custom DAX measures for complex calculations including gross margin percentages and time-based sales trends.  
Visualization: Utilized Waterfall charts, hourly trend lines, and heat maps to represent data clearly and effectively.  
Stack: Power BI Desktop, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Power Query.  

💡 Benefits of this Project

Operational Efficiency: Identifying peak hours (like 7 PM) allows for better resource allocation and targeted marketing efforts.  
Strategic Growth: Highlighting top-performing cities and product categories helps in making data-driven decisions for business expansion.  
Enhanced Reporting: Replaces static spreadsheets with a scalable, real-time reporting environment that grows with the business.  
Customer Insights: Provides a clear understanding of payment trends and customer demographics to tailor the shopping experience.
